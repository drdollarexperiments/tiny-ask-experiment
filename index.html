The Tiny Ask Experiment

What happens when people with substantial publicly reported wealth are asked for a deliberately tiny amount of money? This project is testing that question with requests between $1 and $10.

What is being tested?

Request size

Participants are assigned requests of $1, $2, $5, or $10.

Participation

The experiment compares how often people choose to participate at different request sizes.

Wealth ranges

Publicly available estimates are used to place prospects into broad wealth ranges for aggregate analysis.

Aggregate results

The goal is to report patterns, not publicly identify or shame people who decline.

Where does the money go?

Money received first offsets the direct costs of running the experiment. Any remaining proceeds belong to the project creator. There is no hidden charitable cause behind the request—the experiment itself is the point.

What this is not

This is not a charity, nonprofit fundraiser, academic study, investment opportunity, or claim of financial hardship. It is an independent personal experiment about responses to unusually small financial requests.

Privacy

Individual participation will not be publicly attributed without permission. This page does not use advertising trackers or collect information through a form. Payment processing is handled by PayPal.

Why was I contacted?

Prospects are selected using publicly available information indicating substantial estimated net worth and a legitimate public or professional contact route. No hacked, leaked, or private contact database is used.

The Tiny Ask Experiment

What happens when people with substantial publicly reported wealth are asked for a deliberately tiny amount of money? This project is testing that question with requests between $1 and $10.

What is being tested?

Request size

Participants are assigned small requests of $1, $2, $5, or $10.

Participation

The experiment compares how often people choose to participate at different request sizes.

Wealth bands

Publicly available estimates are used to place prospects into broad wealth ranges for aggregate analysis.

Aggregate results

The goal is to report patterns, not publicly shame or identify individual people who decline.

What this is not

This is not a charity, nonprofit fundraiser, academic study, investment opportunity, or claim of financial hardship. It is an independent personal experiment about how people respond to unusually small financial requests.

Privacy

Individual participation will not be publicly attributed without permission. The site itself does not use advertising trackers or collect information through a form. Payment processing is handled by the payment provider you choose.

Why was I contacted?

Prospects are selected using publicly available information indicating substantial estimated net worth and a legitimate public or professional contact route. No hacked, leaked, or private contact database is part of the experiment.

How the URL works

An experiment link may contain an assigned amount and anonymous participant ID, for example ?amount=5&id=A0371. The ID is only used to organize experimental results.
